Infrastructure platform for disconnected DoD sites

I co-developed a Go platform that provisions, upgrades and backs up Kubernetes clusters and VMs across AWS GovCloud, Azure Government and vSphere, including sites with no internet connection.

I designed and built its air-gap delivery system, which bundles every OS package, container image and Helm chart a site needs into a signed offline bundle, with an optional FIPS mode. I also built the orchestration that deploys FreeIPA, HAProxy and Sonatype Nexus across nodes, and automated 136 CIS and RHEL STIG hardening checks with remediation.
